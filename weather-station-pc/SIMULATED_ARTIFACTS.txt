SIMULATED TRAINING ARTIFACTS — READ BEFORE USE

The database and model in this folder were generated from the project's
deterministic software sensor simulator. They are NOT real Sense HAT readings,
not measurements from your room, and not evidence of real-world forecast
accuracy.

PC:
- data/simulated_training.db: 1,800 simulated observations.
- models/temperature_model.json: model trained from those simulated observations.
- models/temperature_model.report.json: chronological validation/test metrics.
- models/temperature_model.cases.json: held-out test cases.

Raspberry Pi:
- models/temperature_model.json: copy of the same simulated-data model for
  testing the local inference runtime.

For your final assessment, clearly identify these as simulated results. Replace
them with a model trained from real Sense HAT data if the guide/assessment
requires real measurements. Do not mix simulated and real rows in one training
run.
